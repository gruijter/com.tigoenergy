# Tigo Energy

Connect your Tigo Inverter to Homey and get detailed insights into your solar energy production.
Monitor your current power output, as well as your total generation. With the Tigo app for Homey, you can use this data to optimize your energy usage, create Flows, and keep track of your system's performance — all in one place.
Add each solar panel as its own device to see its power and today's yield.
A Tigo EI Premium or Professional subscription is required.
